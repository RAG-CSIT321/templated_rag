Help Guide

Welcome to ChatWithMe

Your AI assistant for tech product recommendations and comparisons. Get personalized advice about laptops, smartphones, and other tech devices to make informed decisions.

Getting Started

  • New Chat: Click the yellow "+" button in the sidebar to start a fresh conversation
  • Quick Start: Use the suggested prompts on the welcome screen for common questions
  • Ask Questions: Type in the input field at the bottom and press Enter to send
  • Multi-line: Use Shift + Enter to add line breaks in your message

Managing Your Chats

  • View History: All your conversations are saved in the sidebar
  • Switch Chats: Click any chat in the sidebar to view that conversation
  • Delete Chat: Hover over a chat and click the trash icon to remove it
  • Sidebar: Toggle the sidebar using the menu icon for more space

Best Practices

  • Be Specific: Include details like budget, preferences, and use case in your questions
  • Follow-up: Ask follow-up questions to get more detailed information
  • Compare: Ask about specific product comparisons for better recommendations
  • Stay Updated: Check back regularly as our product database is updated frequently

Example Questions

"What's the best gaming laptop under $1500 with good battery life?"
"Compare MacBook Air M2 vs Dell XPS 13 for programming"
"Recommend a phone with great camera and battery under $800"
"What are the pros and cons of the latest iPhone?"
